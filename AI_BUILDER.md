## 1. THE SPINE — the one thing everything serves

> **"I was a man who had no reason to keep going — and I got my life back.
> Now the whole point is to walk other people from where I was to where I am."**

Every line of copy, every page, every image is a *vehicle* for that sentence. When a choice is
unclear, return to it. This site is not a brochure for a method — it is the front door to a man
who has been to the bottom and came back, and now walks alongside others doing the same.

**The posture:** not the guru on the mountain — the guy one step ahead on the same trail.

## 2. What the work / site / offer are really about

- **The work** is about *getting your life back.* It is NOT about ayahuasca, and not even about diets
  in the abstract — the **Diet is the path that makes the change hold.** "Experienced, not sold."
- **The site** has one job: **prove he's the real one.** The market is full of glossy "plant medicine
  changed my life" hype. This site is the opposite — quiet, honest, unglamorous truth. It is a
  **filter, not a funnel**: it should repel the festival/shortcut crowd and attract the serious person
  who has built a life and is quietly suffering.
- **The offer is the walk, not the brew:**
  - **Clarity Session ($500)** — the door. One honest conversation. Often that's enough.
  - **Traditional Amazonian Diets ($2,500, separate from the maestro's fee)** — the hero deep work,
    held by Don Alberto, plus a full year of integration that makes it land.
  - Never pitch "come do ayahuasca." Pitch the transformation and the discipline that makes it stick.

## 4. HARD RULES (never break — legal + brand safety)

1. **"Ayahuasca" must NEVER appear on this storefront site.** Use **"Traditional Amazonian Diets."**
   (Editorial surfaces like YouTube/newsletter MAY name it once in his story — but NOT this site,
   because this site is where payment processors and ad platforms read intent.)
2. **No selling the brew.** The offer is the guidance, the diet, the integration — never access to a
   substance. This is what keeps Stripe/Meta/YouTube safe and keeps him out of legal exposure.
3. **No titles.** Not a guru, shaman, healer, psychic, or medium. He is a guide / a man one step ahead.
4. **Fees stay separate.** Claudiu's fee and Don Alberto's fee are NEVER combined into one number.
5. **"RETREAT" is banned as a name for our own offer.** (Naming retreats a client already did is fine.)
6. **Free knowledge is free.** Only personal time is paid. The writing, videos, community stay open.
7. **The suicide truth, if used, carries a support line** in the page footer or nearby
   (e.g. findahelpline.com). It's right, and platforms favor it.
8. **PRIVATE — never on the site:** any cybercrime / prison history. (Legally expunged; clean break 2019.)
   The public arc is the *inner* collapse and the turn, which is universal and stronger.

  theholistic.beehiiv.com (community/newsletter), Instagram @theholisticio.

  matching nod on the site so a visitor arriving from YouTube feels continuity, not whiplash.

## 6. Voice (how it must sound)

Lead with confession, not authority. Honesty is the product. Short, plain, human sentences. Give the
reader permission to leave. Walk alongside, never lead from above. Reframe: it's all the same thing
wearing different clothes. End personal pieces with **"Your friend in this — C."**

Avoid: hype, hustle language, "transform your life in X days," guarantees of outcomes, spiritual
jargon, anything that sounds like a funnel or a launch.
