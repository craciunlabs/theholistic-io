## Item 1 — REQUIRED: Homepage card 03 ("Eight years") fix

**Where:** homepage card 03 — the "Rooted in tradition" credibility card.

**Current copy (to replace):**
> "Eight years with Traditional Amazonian Diets, alongside a maestro of 50+ years."

**Why change it:**
1. **Privacy:** "Eight years" is a precise personal year-count. The brand avoids personal tenure figures on
   public surfaces.
2. **It undersells.** Two stronger, safer anchors exist: the maestro's 50+ years (primary, already on the
   site) and the medicine FAMILY's well-over-a-century of *combined* experience. "A medicine family, not
   one man." Always framed as combined/estimated — never one person's tenure, never a precise audited count.

**Suggested new copy (tune rhythm to the card's voice):**
> "Rooted in a medicine family carrying well over a century of combined experience — led by a maestro of
> 50+ years in Peru. Real tradition, not spiritual tourism."

**Guardrails:**
- Keep "50+ years" for the maestro (verified, safe primary anchor).
- "Well over a century of combined" = the family/team, estimated — never a precise count, never tied to
  one person.
- Do NOT reintroduce any personal year-count for Claudiu anywhere on the site.
- No fees, no "retreat," no guru/shaman/healer title.

### ⚠ CRITICAL WORD RULE — never "diagnose" (or "diagnosis / treat / cure / heal you / patient")

Claudiu is **not** a doctor or clinician. "Diagnose" is a clinical/medical word; on a public page it reads
as a medical claim — a real legal + positioning risk. Use perception/reflection language instead:
"I reflect back what I see," "I name what I feel," "I say plainly what I sense," "I show you what I
notice." The capability (seeing clearly into what's going on) comes through WITHOUT any medical or
guru/psychic/medium title. **This applies anywhere on the site, not just the clarity page.**

**Guardrails:**
- Walk-alongside, not lead-from-above. No medical words (above), no psychic/medium/guru/healer self-title.
- The reader should feel SEEN and left in their own authority — not told what's true.
- Keep any existing graduation line ("the aim was never for you to need me") — this harmonizes with it.
