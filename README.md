# theholistic.io

Brand home for **The Holistic** — business strategy and deep healing for intuitives, empaths, and holistic professionals.

**Live site:** [theholistic.io](https://theholistic.io)

## About

The Holistic is not a course. Not a coaching programme. Not a spiritual brand.

It's a free body of knowledge — built on over two decades in technology and business (started at 15), 8 years of traditional Amazonian plant medicine work, and the conviction that declarative knowledge belongs to everyone.

**Founder:** Claudiu Craciun — [claudiucraciun.com](https://claudiucraciun.com)

## Design System

- **Background:** `#0D0C0A` (near-black)
- **Primary gold:** `#C9A84C` / hover `#E8C96A`
- **Typography:** Cormorant Garant (display serif) + Switzer (body sans)
- **Aesthetic:** Luxury editorial. No spiritual clichés. No guru positioning.

## Brand Pillars

1. **No titles** — Not a guru. Not a shaman. A person who pays attention and shares what he's learned.
2. **Free knowledge** — All content free. Personal time monetised only.
3. **Both worlds** — Tech/business rigour meets traditional plant medicine. Research-backed, grounded in experience.
